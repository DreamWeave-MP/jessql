# Provenance policy

**Status: proposed, for maintainer approval. It is an engineering rule, not legal advice.**

JessQL is licensed `MIT OR Apache-2.0`. A user may take it under either, so everything in the
repository must be distributable under both.

## What is in the repository today

- Source written for this project by its contributors.
- No vendored third-party source. Dependencies are Jess packages pinned by digest in `jess.lock`,
  not copied in.
- A search of `src`, `bench` and `scripts` for attribution markers (`copyright`, `adapted from`,
  `ported from`, `based on`, `derived from`) finds none. That is a search, not an audit.

## Rules

1. **Ideas are free; expression is not.** Reading papers, documentation and other engines'
   designs is expected. Copying or closely translating their source is a provenance event and
   follows rule 3, whatever language it is translated into.
2. **No indiscriminate copying.** Code is not pasted from another project, a forum answer or a
   generated suggestion that reproduces one, without knowing where it came from and its license.
3. **Third-party source is added only with a record**, in the same commit:
   - what it is, where it came from (URL and revision), and its license;
   - the file header or `NOTICE` text that license requires;
   - what was changed.
4. **Licenses that can be combined with `MIT OR Apache-2.0`, with their notice kept:** MIT,
   BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, the PostgreSQL License, Apache-2.0 (with its `NOTICE`, and
   noting that the copied part is then Apache-2.0 only unless its author also offers MIT), and
   material dedicated to the public domain or under CC0.
5. **Not accepted as source:** GPL, LGPL, AGPL, SSPL, BSL, "source-available" and unlicensed
   code. Their published designs may be studied under rule 1.
6. **Test data and fixtures are invented or generated by this repository's own code.** A dataset
   from elsewhere needs the same record as source.
7. **Native code proposed for l3i** follows l3i's own rules as well as these.

## Review checklist for a change that adds outside material

- [ ] The origin and revision are named in the commit and in the file.
- [ ] The license is on the list in rule 4, and its notice text is present.
- [ ] `NOTICE` is added or updated if the license requires one.
- [ ] The material is the smallest that does the job, and what was modified is stated.
- [ ] A reviewer other than the author has compared the record with the origin.
- [ ] If it is a design taken from a paper or another engine, the document that describes the
      feature cites it.
